Korean Music Charts

Who ranked Korean songs, with what, and how — from the first gramophone recordings of 1906 to digital charts

This site traces, on one timeline from 1906, who ranked Korean popular songs and how. No. 1 entries are checked against newspapers of the time or copies of association releases, and each row records its check status. It publishes no lyrics, audio, or album covers, and builds no measure comparing singers' popularity.

Six chapters

Chapter 0. Ways of ranking songsKorean popular song rankings are not one chart running for 120 years.Chapter 1. The age of catalogs: popularity without rankings (1906–1945)No sales ranking is found for the gramophone era.Chapter 2. The age of awards (1945–1980)Before 1981, "rankings" were mostly year-end awards that picked singers and songs once a year.Chapter 3. Weekly chart shows (1981–1998)From 1981 to 1998 the center of weekly television rankings was KBS *Gayo Top 10*.Chapter 4. Sales tallies and gaps (1998–2010)From August 1998 to September 2008 the official sales figure was a monthly tally compiled by the industry association from label reports.Chapter 5. Official charts and platforms (2010–)After 2010, a ranking became a calculation that adds usage and sales across several music services at set weights.
