Method

Source grades

Grade Meaning Example
Primary Original texts of the time; copies of association releases Samcheolli, Oct 1935; Internet Archive copies of MIAK monthly tables
Contemporary report Newspaper or magazine articles from the same period Dong-A Ilbo, 10 Feb 1981, on the launch of Gayo Top 10
Secondary Encyclopedias; articles written decades later Wikipedia lists

Secondary sources are used for checking only, never cited as evidence. In the figures, spans confirmed only by secondary sources are hatched.

Checking the No. 1 lists

  • MIAK monthly No. 1 Korean album (Aug 1998–Sep 2008). The No. 1 was copied from a copy of the table for all 122 months and checked against the Wikipedia list for the same month.
  • KBS Gayo Top 10 weekly No. 1 (1981–1998). The 889-row Wikipedia list is the frame. The weeks to check were fixed before looking at the data: the first broadcast of each month, or the second if the first was skipped. Any year with a mismatch was to be checked in full. Newspapers of the time were read through public search results (opening lines) of the Dong-A Ilbo digital archive. So far 18 of the 203 sample weeks have been checked. The 889 rows of the list cover 888 broadcast dates (one tie on 25 Dec 1991).
  • Each row is marked matched, mismatched, or undetermined. A row is undetermined when it cannot be settled which broadcast an article refers to. Undetermined rows are left out of the match-rate denominator.

Genre classes

Each No. 1 album's genre is recorded twice: the label printed on the table at the time, and a later class. A class is assigned only when the printed label maps directly to an entry in the genre dictionary (ballad, dance, rap/hip hop, R&B/soul, rock/band, and so on). Months labeled only gayo (Korean pop) or Local, or given two genres, are left unclassified. No song gets two genres.

Titles and names

Korean pages use original Korean titles and names. English pages use the official English form where one exists; otherwise the Revised Romanization of Korean, followed by the Korean original in parentheses.

Rights and scope

  • Only facts such as titles, artists, ranks, dates, and sales are used. No lyrics, audio, album covers, broadcast images, or newspaper page images.
  • The MIAK tables carry the line "This material may not be processed or edited without consulting the association." This site does not reproduce the ranking tables. For each month it gives only the No. 1 album and artist and that month's sales, with the address of the copy.
  • No No. 1 list is built for Circle Chart until its terms of use are confirmed.
  • No automated collection program was run against any site. Sources were opened and copied by hand.
  • The site does not discuss allegations of chart manipulation against any singer or agency, and builds no measure comparing singers' popularity.
