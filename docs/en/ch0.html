Chapter 0. Ways of ranking songs

Key point. Korean popular song rankings are not one chart running for 120 years. Magazine polls, year-end awards, broadcast voter panels, label sales reports, and streaming usage each took the role in turn. Between them lie a long stretch with no regular chart found and several shorter gaps.

Four kinds of tally

This site sorts the bodies that ranked songs, and how they did it, into four kinds.

  • Awards and polls. Singers or songs are picked once a year. Examples are the 1935 readers' poll in the magazine Samcheolli, the Radio Seoul song awards from 1965, and MBC's Top 10 Singers, found from about 1966.
  • Broadcast charts. A broadcaster announces a ranking every week. The first case confirmed in print is the weekly top 10 on Dong-A Broadcasting radio (DBS) in 1972. Television chart shows run from KBS Gayo Top 10 in 1981.
  • Sales tallies. Sales figures are collected and ranked. The monthly tally of the record industry association (1998–2008) and the private Hanteo Chart (from 1993) belong here.
  • Digital and official charts. Usage on music services is added up. Gaon Chart launched as the official chart in 2010 and became Circle Chart in 2022. Platform charts such as Melon and the Billboard K-pop Hot 100 are also placed here.

What this site does and does not do

  • It checks the No. 1 entries of chart lists against newspapers of the time or copies of association releases, and marks each row as matched, mismatched, or undetermined.
  • Secondary lists (such as Wikipedia) are used only for checking, never cited as evidence.
  • It does not publish lyrics, audio, album covers, or broadcast images. Only facts such as titles, artist names, ranks, and dates are used.
  • It does not discuss allegations of chart manipulation against any singer or agency, and it builds no measure that compares or rates singers' popularity.

Sources

  • Sources for each system are listed in the data files systems.csv and fig_f1_bands.csv.
  • Grades: primary (copies of association releases, original texts of the time), contemporary report (newspaper articles from the same period), secondary (encyclopedias, articles written decades later).
